Trendline breaks (context only)
  • Timeframes: weekly, daily, 65m. "s" = short horizon, "b" = broader horizon. Lines join the two latest causally confirmed swing highs (descending resistance, R) or lows (rising support, S).
  • A break needs a completed-bar close beyond the line by 0.25×ATR14; later bars mark holding / retest held / failed. Partial periods are excluded; 65m bars are built from 5m regular-session data anchored at the open.
  • Not part of the filter-fit score or pass status. A break is not proof a trend has reversed. n/a = data unavailable or history too short.
How the filter-fit score works (0–100)
  • Support (20): full if price is −3% to +5% vs nearest support (50d/200d MA, 52w low, 3m swing low); fades to 0 at 10 pts outside.
  • IV rank (20): full at 35, linear to 0 at 0 or 70. >60 flagged “premium too rich”, <15 “cheap but possibly no catalyst”.
  • Upside (15): % to average analyst target, full at +50%; 0 unless consensus is Buy or Strong Buy.
  • Pullback (15): deeper of 1m/3m drawdown. Full at −25%, 60% at −10%/−40%, fades to 0 outside; 0 if down 50%+ in a month.
  • Liquidity (30): near-ATM call in the nearest expiry ≥7 days: OI (15, log scale, full at 5,000) + spread (15, full at ≤5% of mid, 0 at ≥25%). “Liquid” = spread ≤10% and OI ≥500.
Δ≈0.45 strike uses Black-Scholes delta from the chain IV. Implied move = ATM IV × √(days/365). ⚑E = earnings date falls before that expiry. Numbers only — the score measures fit to your criteria, not future returns.
