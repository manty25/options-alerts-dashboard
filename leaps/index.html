Trendline breaks (context only)
  • Timeframes: monthly, weekly, daily. Each column shows one compact chip: the highest-scoring latest break across the short (“s”) and broad (“b”) lines. Lines join the two latest causally confirmed swing highs (descending resistance, ↑) or lows (rising support, ↓).
  • Chip labels: Break (close just confirmed), Hold (still beyond the line), Retest (retested and closed beyond), Failed (failed to hold), or “—” if there is no close-confirmed break. n/a = data unavailable or history too short. The number is the 0–100 quality score. Color follows direction (green ↑ / red ↓) and strength (strong ≥75, moderate 50–74, weak <50). A left edge mark highlights a fresh Break. ≠ means the other horizon differs in direction or state — hover for that line, click the chip for full short/broad detail.
  • A break needs a completed-bar close beyond the line by 0.25×ATR14. Partial periods are excluded.
  • Quality score: touches (max 40) + time held, per-timeframe tiers (max 35) + break volume (max 25); failed to hold = 0. “Active break on” filters to Break or Retest on the selected timeframe(s).
  • Not part of the filter-fit score, pass status or sort. A break is not proof a trend has reversed.
How the filter-fit score works (0–100)
  • Support (25): full if price is −3% to +5% vs nearest support (50d/200d MA, 52w low, 3m swing low); fades to 0 at 10 pts outside that band.
  • IV rank (20): full at 35 (center of your 20–50 band), linear to 0 at 0 or 70. >60 flagged “premium too rich”, <15 “cheap but possibly no catalyst”.
  • Upside (20): % to average analyst target, scaled to full at +50%; 0 unless consensus is Buy or Strong Buy.
  • Pullback (20): deeper of 1m/3m drawdown from high. Full at −25%, 60% at the −10%/−40% edges, fades to 0 at 10 pts outside; 0 if down 50%+ in a month.
  • Liquidity (15): sample Apr–Jul 2027 call: OI (10, log scale, full at 5,000) + bid/ask spread (5, full at 0%, 0 at ≥20% of mid).
The score only measures fit to your own criteria. It says nothing about future returns.
