# How PR campaigns are run today (research basis)

This is the research the app's design rests on. Figures come from industry surveys and vendor
price lists; most of them are published by vendors, so read them as directional. Sources are at the end.

## 1. The agency workflow

A typical agency or in-house campaign runs in seven phases. The time and cost sit mostly in
phases 3–5, which are manual.

| # | Phase | What happens | Typical time | Main cost |
|---|-------|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | Brief & goals | Client intake, business objective, audience, key messages, "why now" | 1–5 days | Account team hours |
| 2 | News judgment | Is it news? Find the angle (first, biggest, data, local, human, trend, conflict) | 1–3 days | Senior strategist hours |
| 3 | Asset creation | Press release (AP style, inverted pyramid), pitch emails, media kit, FAQ, social copy, exec quotes | 3–10 days incl. approvals | Writer hours + review rounds |
| 4 | Media list | Build a list in Cision / Muck Rack / Meltwater by beat, then hand-trim | 1–3 days | $5k–$15k/yr database seat |
| 5 | Outreach | Exclusive or embargo to tier-1 first, then personalised one-to-one pitches, one follow-up | 1–2 weeks | Account exec hours |
| 6 | Distribution | Newswire (PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire, EIN Presswire…), owned newsroom, social | Same day | $100–$1,500+ per release |
| 7 | Monitoring & reporting | Clip tracking, reach, sentiment, share of voice, monthly report | Ongoing | Monitoring seat + analyst hours |

Typical all-in cost of one product-launch campaign: agency retainer **$5k–$25k/month** (US mid-market),
a wire release at **$400–$1,500** for national US distribution, and monitoring/database seats. A first draft of a
release usually takes **one to two weeks** from brief to distribution.

## 2. What journalists say they want

* **Relevance is the top filter.** In Cision's 2025 State of the Media survey (3,000+ journalists), a lack of
  relevance was the most common reason to reject a pitch (86%), and 78% said they would block a PR contact
  who keeps sending off-beat pitches. Marketing-speak (~58%), unsourced claims (~56%) and repeated follow-ups (~52%)
  also lead to blocks.
* **Email, one-to-one, short.** Email is still the dominant channel. Muck Rack surveys put the ideal pitch under
  **200 words**, and most journalists prefer to receive pitches **before noon** in their time zone.
* **Volume is high.** About half of journalists receive 50+ pitches a week, so a subject line has to state the news.
* **Exclusives work.** Subject lines that offer an exclusive get higher open and reply rates (BuzzStream data).
* **They want sources, data and assets.** The thing journalists value most from PR people is a connection to relevant
  sources (63%). Data, original research, images and an available spokesperson all raise the chance of coverage.
* **One follow-up, not three.** Repeated nudges are a blocking trigger.

## 3. Press release distribution services

| Tier | Examples | 2026 list price (approx.) | What you get | When it is worth it |
|------|----------|---------------------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Tier-1 wire | PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire | $350–$1,500+ per release (+ membership fees, word-count and media add-ons) | AP/Bloomberg/Reuters terminals, regulatory disclosure, broad syndication | Public-company material news (Reg FD / MAR disclosure), earnings, M&A |
| Value wire | EIN Presswire, PRWeb, Accesswire, Newswire.com | $99–$400 | Syndication to partner sites, Google News, some trade feeds | SEO / AI-search footprint, small companies on a budget |
| Budget / free | IssueWire, OpenPR, PR.com, owned newsroom | $0–$50 | Indexable page | Backlinks and discoverability only |
| Science | EurekAlert!, AlphaGalileo | Institutional subscription | Science reporters with embargo support | Peer-reviewed research |

Key point: **wire distribution mostly buys syndication and indexing, not journalist attention.** Coverage that
journalists choose to write comes from targeted one-to-one pitches. A wire is required only for regulatory disclosure.

## 4. AI search has changed the value of a release

Muck Rack's *Generative Pulse* / *What Is AI Reading?* studies (2025) found that most links cited by ChatGPT,
Claude and Gemini come from **earned (non-paid) media**: 84–94% depending on the edition, with paid content
around 0.3%. Press releases and newsroom pages with **statistics, bullet points and plain, objective language**
are cited more often. So a modern campaign must also produce:

* a crawlable newsroom page with `NewsArticle` structured data and an RSS feed,
* a fact sheet in plain, quotable sentences (numbers, dates, names),
* consistent facts across every asset, so models see the same claims everywhere.

What agencies use for each kind of story. Channels in **bold** are the main ones.

| Topic | Primary media | Secondary / amplification | Wire? | Notes |
|-------|---------------|---------------------------|-------|-------|
| Product launch (tech/SaaS) | **Tech & trade press, exclusive to one tier-1** | LinkedIn, X, Hacker News, Product Hunt, Reddit (relevant subreddits), newsletters | Value wire optional | Demo access and screenshots matter |
| Funding round | **Business/startup press (exclusive)** | LinkedIn, X, investor amplification | Value wire for SEO | Exclusive to one outlet the day before is standard |
| Earnings / material financial news | **Tier-1 wire (disclosure)**, financial press | IR site, LinkedIn | **Required** (Reg FD / MAR) | Fixed timing, no exclusives |
| M&A | **Tier-1 wire**, business & trade press | LinkedIn, employee comms | Usually required | Coordinated with legal |
| Executive hire | Trade press, local business journals | LinkedIn (very strong) | Optional | Personnel columns |
| Partnership | Trade press of both partners | Both companies' LinkedIn and X | Optional | Joint quotes |
| Research / data report | **National + trade press, data journalists** | LinkedIn, X, Reddit (data subs), newsletters | Value wire | Charts and methodology are what make it pitchable |
| Scientific study | **Science desks via EurekAlert!**, The Conversation | X/Bluesky science communities | Science service | Embargo tied to journal publication |
| Health / medical | Health trade (STAT, Fierce), consumer health desks | LinkedIn, patient communities | Value or tier-1 | Medical-claims review required |
| Consumer product | **Lifestyle, gift guides, creators** | Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest | Optional | Samples and imagery are what make it pitchable |
| Event | Local media, calendar listings, trade press | Social, community calendars | No | Media advisory (who/what/when/where) |
| Award / recognition | Trade press, local press | LinkedIn | Optional | Low news value; keep short |
| Nonprofit / cause | Local press, op-eds, cause communities | Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn | No | Human story first |
| Local / community | **Local TV, radio, newspapers, Patch** | Nextdoor, Facebook groups | No | Visuals and a local spokesperson |
| Crisis / statement | **Direct to reporters covering the story**, owned newsroom | Holding statement on owned channels | **Never** | Speed and accuracy over reach |
| Gaming / entertainment | Gaming/entertainment press, creators | Discord, Reddit, Twitch, YouTube, TikTok | Optional | Keys, trailers, embargo |
| Policy / public affairs | Politics & policy desks, trade press | LinkedIn, X | Optional | Coalition statements |

## 6. Timing conventions

* Pitch **Tuesday–Thursday**, **06:00–10:00** in the recipient's time zone; avoid Mondays (inbox backlog), Friday
  afternoons, holidays and major scheduled news.
* Schedule wire releases **off the hour** (e.g. 08:05, not 08:00), because the top of the hour is crowded.
* Exclusive: offer **3–7 days** before launch with a clear deadline; if declined, move to the next outlet.
* Embargo: send **24–72 hours** before the lift time; give the lift time in the reporter's own time zone.
* Follow up **once**, 3–4 business days later, with something new (data point, asset, availability).

## 7. Measurement

The **Barcelona Principles 3.0** (AMEC) are the industry standard: measure outcomes, not just outputs; no
Advertising Value Equivalents (AVE); be transparent about method. Agencies report:

* Outputs — releases sent, pitches sent, posts published
* Outtakes — opens (unreliable since Apple Mail Privacy Protection), clicks, replies, interview requests
* Outcomes — placements, tier-weighted reach, key-message pull-through, sentiment, share of voice, backlinks,
  referral traffic, AI-answer citations, business actions (sign-ups, leads)

## 8. Where current tools fall short (the gap the app targets)

| Problem | Today | Target for this app |
|---------|-------|---------------------|
| **Speed** | 1–2 weeks brief→launch | Brief→approved campaign kit in minutes; launch same day |
| **Cost** | $5k–$25k/month + $400–$1,500 per wire + $5k–$15k/yr database | Pay-per-campaign AI cost (cents to dollars); wire only when the topic needs it |
| **Reach quality** | Spray-and-pray lists; wire syndication that few people read | Recency-based targeting (who covered this topic in the last 90 days), one reporter per outlet, personalised pitches |
| **AI search** | Rarely addressed | Fact sheet, structured data, consistent claims across assets |
| **Measurement** | Clip counts, AVE | Barcelona-aligned report with cost per placement and time to first coverage |
